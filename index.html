Responsible science

Safety & ethics come first

Cellsius exists to make biology approachable — and that means doing it responsibly. Every product we sell is chosen for education and beginner safety.

We do not sell human pathogens, controlled organisms, or anything intended for use in or on the human body. Our kits use the same non-pathogenic strains found in school and university teaching labs.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this safe to do at home?

Yes. Our kits use non-pathogenic, biosafety level 1 (BSL-1) organisms — the same harmless strains used in classrooms worldwide. Each kit includes a safety guide covering handling, cleanup, and proper disposal. We recommend users be 16+ or supervised by an adult.

Do I need any prior experience or equipment?

No prior experience is needed — our guides start from the basics. Beginner kits include everything you need. If you want to run more advanced experiments, our Equipment section has affordable pipettes, incubators, and centrifuges.

Is genetic engineering at home legal?

In most countries, working with non-pathogenic organisms for personal education is legal. Regulations vary by region, so we ask customers to follow their local laws. We do not ship regulated organisms and never sell anything for use in humans or animals.

Yes — educators get discounted multi-student bundles and free lesson plans. Reach out through the educator link in the Learn section and we'll set you up.
