Responsible science

Safety & ethics come first

Cellsius exists to make biology approachable — and that means doing it responsibly. Every product we list or sell is chosen for education and beginner safety.

We do not sell or promote human pathogens, controlled organisms, or anything intended for use in or on the human body. Our picks use the same non-pathogenic strains found in school and university teaching labs.

Questions

Frequently asked

What's the difference between the Shop and Our Kits?

Shop items are curated picks from trusted makers and retailers — you buy on their site through an affiliate link, and we may earn a small commission. Our Kits are made by Cellsius and sold here directly with secure card checkout.

Is this safe to do at home?

Yes. Everything we list uses non-pathogenic, biosafety level 1 (BSL-1) organisms — the same harmless strains used in classrooms worldwide. Each kit includes safety and disposal guidance. We recommend users be 16+ or supervised by an adult.

Do I need prior experience or equipment?

No. The skill tree starts from the very basics and tells you exactly what to learn and buy next. Beginner kits include everything you need; the Equipment section covers affordable gear when you're ready for more.

Is genetic engineering at home legal?

In most countries, working with non-pathogenic organisms for personal education is legal. Rules vary by region, so please follow your local laws. We never list regulated organisms or anything for use in humans or animals.

How does affiliate pricing work?

Affiliate links cost you nothing extra — the price is the vendor's normal price, and we receive a small commission that helps keep our guides free. Prices shown here are indicative; the live price is always on the vendor's page.

How does payment work for Our Kits?

Checkout is handled by Stripe, so your card details never touch our servers. This demo storefront runs in "demo mode" until Stripe keys are added — buttons fall back to an email order in the meantime.
