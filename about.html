Our mission

Biology belongs to everyone.

For most of history, doing real molecular biology meant a university, a grant, and a locked door. Cellsius exists to change that — safely, openly, and one skill at a time.

The mission

What we're here to do

We make the tools and knowledge of modern biology accessible to anyone curious enough to try — the student, the teacher, the tinkerer, the community lab — while keeping it safe and keeping the science open.

Why we exist

The story

Cellsius started from a simple frustration: the most exciting science of our era — genetic engineering, CRISPR, synthetic biology — felt sealed off. The knowledge lived in journals, the reagents behind institutional accounts, and the encouragement almost nowhere. Meanwhile a generation of curious people had the internet, a kitchen table, and no idea where to start.

So we built two things. First, a skill tree: a clear, gamified path that turns "biology" from an intimidating wall into a series of unlockable, teachable steps. Second, a curated shop that removes the other barrier — knowing what to buy. We test and hand-pick kits from trusted makers, point you to the right lab staples, and make a couple of our own on-ramp products. No 10,000-SKU catalog. Just the right next thing.

We're deliberately not a faceless marketplace. Every product ties back to a skill, every skill ties back to a free protocol, and the whole thing is designed so a total beginner can go from their first petri dish to a real CRISPR edit — understanding why at every step.

Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

Cellsius keeps its guides and protocols free in part through affiliate partnerships. Some links in our shop and skill tree — including Amazon and independent makers — are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you: you pay the vendor's normal price.

As an Amazon Associate, Cellsius earns from qualifying purchases. This never changes what we recommend. We list products because we think they're the best way to learn a skill — not because of commission. Prices shown on our site are indicative. The live, authoritative price is always on the vendor's own page.

Our own products (in "Our Kits") are sold directly by Cellsius with card payments processed securely by Stripe — your card details never touch our servers.
